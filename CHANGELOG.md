# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- Support for the Fool: Explosive Arrows, Covering Fire, All Is Well and other powers and relics that react to arrows or created cards are credited by name, instead of to the card that set them off or "Unknown source". Covering Fire counts as the Fool's party block.
- Works with the game patch that added the Fool.

## 1.0.1

- Co-op: a player who rejoins a saved run keeps their numbers (before, only the host did).
- Accelerant: the extra poison ticks it causes are credited to Accelerant.
- Noxious Fumes and other start-of-turn powers are named instead of showing as "Unknown source".
- Fight tab: each poisoned enemy shows the poison it takes next turn, Accelerant included. Hover for details.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Run, Fight, Details, Debuffs, Block and Turns tabs.
- Damage bars split by type (HP, block, poison, doom) and sized against the top player.
- Poison and doom credited to whoever applied them, split by their share of the stacks.
- Damage, debuffs and block credited per card, relic, potion, orb and power, including debuffs like Strangle.
- Tabs or Stacked layout; the window can be moved, resized and folded.
- Numbers survive a crash or save-and-quit, and reset on a new run.
- Installer, in-game Update button and Report a bug button.
